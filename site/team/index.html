Meet the team

A shared vision.
A dedicated team.

Our Philippine team provides local service, maintenance and customer support for AlkaEco’s environmental technologies, developed by Dutch innovation engineer Ludwig Marie Louis de Haan, also known as Luc de Haan. We connect Philippine customers with the technical expertise behind these innovations, bringing Dutch engineering and local support together.

From coordinating installation and operator guidance to preventive maintenance, troubleshooting and follow-up support, we help customers address the practical needs of their equipment. We work with AlkaEco’s technical specialists and partners to coordinate the right support, with a focus on careful operation, equipment reliability and long-term care.

AlkaEco’s innovations include alkaline hydrolysis systems for people and animals, energy recall technology, and dedicated drug destruction units. Our role in the Philippines is to support customers throughout their journey, providing a local point of contact for service enquiries, maintenance coordination and ongoing collaboration.
